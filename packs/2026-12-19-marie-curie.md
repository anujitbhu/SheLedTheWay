## On-screen hook
Her country would not let women into university. So she studied in secret.

## Voiceover
Warsaw, the 1880s. Maria Sklodowska is brilliant, and barred from university because she is a woman. So she joins the Flying University, an illegal school that keeps changing rooms to avoid the authorities. She makes a pact with her sister: she will work as a governess to pay for her sister's medical studies, then her sister will pay for hers. In 1891 her turn comes, and she leaves for Paris. In 1903 she becomes the first woman to win a Nobel Prize. In 1911 she wins a second. She is still the only person with Nobel Prizes in two different sciences. She led the way.
