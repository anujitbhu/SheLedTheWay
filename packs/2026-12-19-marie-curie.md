## Voiceover
Warsaw, the 1880s. Marie Curie (Maria Sklodowska) is brilliant, and barred from university because she is a woman. So she joins the Flying University, an illegal school that keeps changing rooms to avoid the authorities. She makes a pact with her sister: she will work as a governess to pay for her sister's medical studies, then her sister will pay for hers. In 1891 her turn comes, and she leaves for Paris. In 1903 she becomes the first woman to win a Nobel Prize. In 1911 she wins a second. She is still the only person with Nobel Prizes in two different sciences. She led the way.

## Caption
Marie Curie studied in secret before becoming the first woman to win a Nobel Prize.

In 1880s Warsaw, Maria Sklodowska was barred from university because she was a woman. She joined the Flying University, an illegal school that changed rooms to avoid the authorities.

She and her sister made a pact: she would work as a governess to fund her sister’s medical studies, and her sister would then pay for hers. In 1891, she left for Paris.

The Nobel Prize followed in 1903, then a second in 1911. She remains the only person with Nobel Prizes in two different sciences.
