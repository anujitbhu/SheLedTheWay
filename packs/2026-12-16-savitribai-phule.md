## On-screen hook

## Voiceover
Pune, 1848. Savitribai Phule is walking to work, and people are throwing stones and cow dung at her. Her offence? Teaching girls to read. She was married at nine and could not read or write herself, until her husband Jyotirao taught her. In 1848 they opened a school for girls at Bhide Wada, and she became its first teacher. So every day she carried a spare sari, changed when she arrived, and taught. She never stopped. If a girl in India opens a schoolbook today, Savitribai walked that road first. She led the way.
