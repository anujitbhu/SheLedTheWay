## Caption
Anandibai Joshi’s medical degree was her answer to a need: women’s healthcare in India needed women doctors.

In 1883, after her infant son died, she left India for Philadelphia and joined the Woman’s Medical College of Pennsylvania. She graduated in 1886. A medical post at Albert Edward Hospital in Kolhapur awaited her, but she became seriously ill before she could begin practice and died on February 26, 1887.

The Library of Congress and Drexel Legacy Center describe her historical “first” in different qualified ways, so this story focuses on her degree, her medical purpose and the work she hoped to do.
