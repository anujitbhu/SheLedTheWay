# 2026-11-23 | Rani Lakshmibai | Courage | Reel

## On-screen hook
Jhansi was annexed after the British refused her adopted son as heir. Lakshmibai kept resisting.

## Voiceover
After her husband died in 1853, Rani Lakshmibai adopted a son, Damodar Rao. The British refused to recognize him as heir, and Jhansi was annexed in February 1854. Lakshmibai appealed against the decision. During the 1857 revolt, she became an authority in Jhansi. When British forces attacked in March 1858, she defended the city, then escaped to Kalpi and later reached Gwalior. Accounts differ on the exact day she died near Gwalior in June 1858, so we will leave the date there. Her resistance became part of Jhansi’s history. She led the way.

## Fact-check notes
Sources agree that Lakshmibai died near Gwalior in June 1858 but differ on the exact day: the Ministry of Culture dictionary dates her death to 17 June, while The Indian Express gives 18 June. PBS notes that reports vary. The script therefore gives no exact day. No quote or disputed “first” claim is used.
