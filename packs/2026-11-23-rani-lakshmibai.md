Jhansi, March 1858. British forces surround the city as Rani Lakshmibai organizes its defense. The East India Company refuses to recognize her husband’s adopted heir and takes control of Jhansi. Her husband had named her regent for Damodar Rao. As the siege closes, she escapes to Kalpi, then reaches Gwalior, where fighting continues. She dies near Gwalior in June 1858. In March 1858, British forces surrounded Jhansi. As the siege closed, she escaped to Kalpi, then reached Gwalior, where the fighting continued. She died near Gwalior in June 1858.

## Fact-check notes
Sources agree that Lakshmibai died near Gwalior in June 1858 but differ on the exact day: the Ministry of Culture dictionary dates her death to 17 June, while IGNCA’s Heroes Who Made History account says 18 June. PBS notes that reports vary. The script therefore gives no exact day. The Ministry dictionary says her husband nominated her as regent of his adopted son; PBS says the Raja’s adopted heir was refused recognition. No quote or disputed “first” claim is used.
