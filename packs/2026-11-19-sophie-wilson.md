## Voiceover
Cambridge, 1980s. At Acorn, Sophie Wilson needs a processor for the company’s next computer, small and economical enough to fit the brief. With Steve Furber, she designs ARM around a reduced instruction set. Years earlier, Wilson had helped build the BBC Micro prototype in less than a week and written its BASIC interpreter. That machine brings computing to classrooms; ARM later powers devices such as Acorn’s Archimedes and Apple’s Newton. One tight design challenge becomes a platform other teams can reuse. She led the way.
