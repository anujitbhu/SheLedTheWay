# 2026-11-19 | Sophie Wilson | Perseverance | Reel

## On-screen hook
Acorn needed a small, efficient processor. Sophie Wilson helped make its design travel far.

## Voiceover
Cambridge, 1980s. Acorn needs a processor for its next computer, small and economical enough to fit the brief. Sophie Wilson and Steve Furber design ARM around a reduced instruction set. Years earlier, Wilson had helped build the BBC Micro prototype in less than a week and written its BASIC interpreter. That machine brings computing to classrooms; ARM later powers devices such as Acorn’s Archimedes and Apple’s Newton. One tight design challenge becomes a platform other teams can reuse. She led the way.
