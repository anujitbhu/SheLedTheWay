# 2026-12-21 | Junko Tabei | Courage | Reel

## On-screen hook
Twelve days before she stood on top of Everest, she was buried by an avalanche.

## Voiceover
May 1975. Junko Tabei and her all-women Japanese team are asleep at 6,300 metres when an avalanche hits their camp. She is buried and knocked unconscious, and Sherpa guides dig her out. She is badly bruised. She keeps climbing. On 16 May 1975, aged 35, with Sherpa Ang Tsering, she becomes the first woman to reach the summit of Everest. In 1992 she becomes the first woman to climb the highest peak on every continent. She said she would rather be remembered as the 36th person up Everest. She led the way.
