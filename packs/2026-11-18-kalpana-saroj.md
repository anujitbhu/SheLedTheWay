## On-screen hook
The workers of a bankrupt factory asked one woman for help. She had left school in class nine.

## Voiceover
Kalpana Saroj was born in 1961 into a Dalit family in a Maharashtra village, and married at twelve. She came to Mumbai and built her own businesses from nothing. Then the workers of Kamani Tubes came to her door. The company was buried under about 116 crore rupees of debt and well over a hundred court cases. Everyone told her to stay away. She took it on. By 2006 she was its chairperson, and she steered it back to profit. In 2013 she received the Padma Shri. She led the way.
