## Slides
1. Nobody would hire her as a brewmaster. So she built a biotech company in a garage.
2. Kiran Mazumdar trained in brewing in Australia, following her father, a head brewmaster.
3. Back in India in the 1970s, breweries would not give the job to a woman.
4. In 1978, aged 25, she started Biocon India in the garage of her rented house in Bengaluru.
5. Her seed capital was 10,000 rupees.
6. Banks turned her down: the business was new to India, and it was run by a woman.
7. Biocon began with industrial enzymes, moved into medicines, and listed on the stock market in 2004.
8. The lesson: when one door stays shut, build your own building. Follow for a new story every day.
