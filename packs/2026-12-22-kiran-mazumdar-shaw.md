## Caption
Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw started Biocon in a garage after breweries would not hire her as a brewmaster.

She trained in brewing in Australia, following her father, a head brewmaster. Back in India in the 1970s, breweries would not give the job to a woman.

In 1978, aged 25, she started Biocon India in the garage of a rented Bengaluru house with 10,000 rupees in seed capital. Banks turned her down: the business was new to India and run by a woman.

Biocon began with industrial enzymes, moved into medicines, and listed in 2004.
