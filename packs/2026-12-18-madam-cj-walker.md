## Caption
Madam C.J. Walker built a hair-products business after working as a laundress for about $1.50 a day.

Born Sarah Breedlove in Louisiana in 1867, she was the first child in her family born free. Widowed by 20 with a toddler, she worked as a laundress before hair loss led her to seek a solution. Around 1905, she launched Madam Walker’s Wonderful Hair Grower and sold it door-to-door.

By 1917, her company said it had trained more than 20,000 women as sales agents. At her death in 1919, her fortune was estimated at more than $1 million. Guinness lists her as the first self-made woman millionaire, though historians note that the “first” is hard to prove.
