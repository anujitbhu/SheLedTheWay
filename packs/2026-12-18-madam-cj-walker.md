# 2026-12-18 | Madam C.J. Walker | Wealth | Carousel

## Slides
1. She earned about $1.50 a day washing clothes. She died a millionaire.
2. Born Sarah Breedlove in Louisiana in 1867, the first child in her family born free.
3. By 20 she was a widow with a toddler, working as a laundress.
4. Then her own hair began to fall out. She went looking for a fix.
5. Around 1905 she launched Madam Walker's Wonderful Hair Grower and sold it door to door.
6. She did not only sell. By 1917 her company said it had trained more than 20,000 women as sales agents.
7. At her death in 1919 her fortune was estimated at over $1 million. Guinness lists her as the first self-made woman millionaire.
8. The lesson: solve your own problem, then teach others to earn from the solution. Follow for a new story every day.

## Caption
