## If this goes public

- **Attribution is required** for every CC-licensed image, including the creator, the license, and a link to the source. The prototype does this in Studio → Facts → Photographs.
- **Share-alike.** Lynn Conway, Xerox PARC and the wafer are CC BY-SA. The duotone treatment is an adaptation. If the interface or a Reel is published, the adapted image must be shared under the same license or replaced with a compatible public-domain image.
- **The brand kit’s stricter rule.** `brand/brand-kit.md` prefers public-domain archive photos. Most of the portraits here are CC BY rather than public domain because there is no public-domain portrait for every subject. This is a prototype exception, recorded deliberately. Replace the CC BY-SA images with public-domain equivalents before a public release if the owner wants the brand rule strictly enforced.
- **No synthetic people.** No AI-generated photograph or AI likeness is used.
- **The chip images are context, not claims.** The die and wafer are labelled as context. They are not photographs of the specific VLSI work in the Lynn Conway story. Keep that caption if the collage is reused.

The source files are in `design/images/source/`; the duotone assets are in `design/images/processed/`. The same credits are rendered in the prototype’s Facts tab from `design/data.js`.
