# Colette Kress source refresh — 14 November 2026

## Findings

NVIDIA announced on 12 November that CFO Colette Kress would serve as interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor; she is to continue as CFO during the transition.[1][2]

The SEC filing dates the board’s acceptance of the outgoing CEO’s resignation to 11 November, while NVIDIA’s public announcement is dated 12 November; describe 12 November as the announcement date, not as the board-action date.[2]

NVIDIA’s materials place Kress at the company as CFO since 2013 and describe nearly 25 years of prior finance work at major technology companies.[1][2]

Reuters and AP independently corroborate her 2013 start and earlier roles including Cisco, Microsoft, and Texas Instruments.[3][4]

The filing gives Kress’s age as 59, but it adds little to this story and should be omitted.[2]

The existing “calm voice explaining the numbers,” “engine of the AI boom,” and “handed the whole company” language is subjective or overstates an expressly interim appointment; remove it for a neutral account.

The existing “her first earnings call in the new role is on 17 November” line is not established by the inspected notices: NVIDIA schedules its results call for that date and says written CFO commentary will precede it, but does not identify Kress as making a first live appearance as interim CEO.[1]

## Recommended factual spine

Say that NVIDIA announced on 12 November that its CFO, Colette Kress, would also serve as interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent successor.[1][2]

Say that she joined in 2013 after nearly 25 years in finance roles at major technology companies.[1][2]

Reuters and AP corroborate her earlier roles, including Cisco, Microsoft, and Texas Instruments.[3][4]

Keep the departure reason out of this Reel; it is not needed to explain Kress’s interim appointment.

Recheck the interim status and successor search on the morning of posting because the story is time-sensitive.
