# Colette Kress voiceover — fact check

## Claim decisions

NVIDIA’s biography says Kress joined in September 2013 after nearly 25 years in finance roles at technology companies, including Cisco, Microsoft and Texas Instruments.[1]

Globes reports that her sons, then eight and ten, encouraged her to accept the NVIDIA job because they knew the company through gaming; it also reports that Jensen Huang recruited her and outlined his vision for NVIDIA.[2]

Present these as circumstances around her decision, not as the sole reason she joined.[2]

I describe NVIDIA as “far smaller then” only relative to its later scale: the company reported $4.13 billion in FY2014 revenue and $215.9 billion in FY2026 revenue.[3][4]

The FY2027 Q2 call identifies Kress as NVIDIA’s CFO and records her presenting results.[5]

The revenue comparison describes company results during her tenure; it does not show that Kress personally caused the growth.[3][4][5]

NVIDIA’s November 12, 2026 transition announcement says Kress would serve as interim CEO while continuing as CFO and that the board would search for a permanent CEO.[6]

Recheck the status on November 16 before publishing.[6]
