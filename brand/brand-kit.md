# Brand kit

## Profile

- Instagram: @SheLedtheWay (Professional, Creator, category Education)
- Name field: She Led The Way: Women in History
- Bio: Daily stories of women who broke barriers, built empires & changed history. Perseverance, Courage, Wealth. A new story every day.
- Format: faceless, English, voiceover in the owner's cloned voice

## Colours

| Role | Colour | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Deep plum | #3B1C4A |
| Accent, logo, pillar tags | Antique gold | #C9A24B |
| Text on dark, light slides | Ivory | #F7F1E8 |
| Highlight words | Terracotta rose | #B5544B |
| Body text on light | Charcoal | #1E1A22 |

Fonts: Playfair Display (headlines), Inter (body). Both are free on Google Fonts.

## Post templates

- Reel cover: her name in Playfair, one-line hook below, pillar tag in gold at the top
- Carousel: slide 1 hook, slides 2 to 7 one fact each, slide 8 the lesson and a follow prompt
- Sunday recap: three lessons from the week on one plum slide
- Every Reel ends on the line "She led the way."

## Weekly rhythm (post 7 pm to 9 pm IST)

Mon Reel, Tue Carousel, Wed Reel, Thu Reel, Fri Carousel, Sat Reel, Sun Recap.

## Tone

- One woman, one story, one lesson per post
- Dates, places and numbers in every story; no vague praise
- No invented quotes; a quote is used only with a checked source
- Where historians dispute a "first", the caption says so
- No current net-worth figures

## Images

- Public-domain archive photos (Wikimedia Commons, Library of Congress) with credit, or text-led and illustrated slides
- No AI-generated "photographs" of real women
- Turn on Instagram's AI label for posts with AI-generated audio or imagery
