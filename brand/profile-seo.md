# Profile settings for search

Instagram and Google read these fields on every search. Set them once in the Instagram app (Edit profile) and keep them stable.

| Field | Set it to | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name (the bold line, not the @handle) | `SheLedTheWay | Women Who Made History` | The most heavily weighted search field. It should say what the page is about in the words people search. |
| Username | `sheledtheway` | Keep as is. |
| Category | Education | Tells Instagram what kind of account this is. |
| Bio | `True stories of women who broke barriers, built empires and changed history. Women leaders, pioneers and founders. A new story every day.` | Bio text is indexed. It names the topics the page should be found for. |
| Account type | Professional (Creator), public | Required for Google to index posts. |
| Search engine indexing | On (Settings, Account privacy, Search engine indexing) | Lets posts appear in Google and Bing. |
| Auto-generated captions | On (Settings, Accessibility) | Captions are read for search and help sound-off viewers. |

Maximum lengths change; if Instagram rejects a field as too long, shorten it but keep the phrase "Women Who Made History" in the name.
